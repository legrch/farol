# Rejected Signal Lens Dock studies

**Owner decision on 2026-10-02: rejected; do not ship.** The three material studies below were too glossy, glamorous and plastic or glass-like. They are preserved only as comparison history. Signal Lens remains a direction for a separate simpler icon iteration. Keep the existing production icon until a replacement is separately approved.

Design review assets, 2 October 2026. Three original generated raster concepts; each PNG is 1254 × 1254 RGBA with transparent outer corners. Originals are retained; these files are copies for the local prototype. No ICNS or installed app bundle was changed.

The common brief is a single abstract Signal Lens: two open curved arcs and a pearl center, sculpted material and multiple coordinated colors, no yellow center, text, literal lamp, lighthouse or face. The three material directions are:

- `iris-glass.png`: translucent Iris and Cobalt glass, luminous pearl center, deep indigo square.
- `prism.png`: pearl glass with cyan, Cobalt and Iris reflections over a satin midnight square.
- `aurora-enamel.png`: Cobalt/Iris enamel with a subtle magenta rim, pearl center, pale porcelain square.

`brand.html` is the review surface, including small sizes and a Dock illustration. These images are concepts rather than optical masters. Before adoption, normalize symbol geometry/insets, clean the outer contours, refine small sizes and verify native app-icon packaging and Dock rendering. The monochrome menu-bar template is an independently controlled vector in `mark-library.js`.
